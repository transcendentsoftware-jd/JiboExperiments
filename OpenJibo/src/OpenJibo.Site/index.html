Self-hosted isolated

Available for preview testing. You operate the server, storage, backups and updates. No managed-cloud account is required. You pay for hardware and configured services. HTTP or HTTPS is a deployment choice, not an authentication bypass.

Self-hosted hybrid

Starter release planned. Owner-fleet-only hosting with HTTPS and trusted-server enrollment. Registration, renewal, revocation and disconnected behavior need acceptance first.

Managed providers

Provider starters planned. HTTPS with controlled multi-owner admission and provider-specific accounts, billing and support. OpenJibo Cloud is Transcendent Software LLC's separate service. This page does not promise public signup or pricing.
